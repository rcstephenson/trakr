**Goal:** Lean muscle
**Priority muscles:** Lateral delts, rear delts, lats/upper back, glute max, glute medius

## Execution Rules

- **Proximity to failure:** Most sets end 0–3 reps short of failure. Isolation work (lateral raises, curls, leg extensions) can go closer; heavy squats and RDLs stay further away.
- **Progression:** Add reps within the listed range first. Once you hit the top of the range on all sets, add weight and drop back to the bottom.
- **Lateral raise technique:** Lead with the elbows, stop around shoulder height, control the lowering phase. Light enough that there's no torso swing.
- **Rest:** 2–3 min on compounds, 60–90 sec on isolation.

---

## Nutrition

A lean bulk is the right call here — muscle mass is the limiting factor for a "ripped" look, not body fat.

- **Calories:** ~250–350 above maintenance per day
- **Rate of gain:** 0.25–0.5% of body weight per month
- **Protein:** 1.6–2.2 g per kg of body weight per day

If you're newer to lifting, eating at maintenance can build muscle while losing fat, just more slowly.
